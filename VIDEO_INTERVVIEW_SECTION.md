# Task 2: Video Technical Response Guidelines

As part of the Data Engineer assessment, candidates are required to record a short video answering five scenario-based questions. This section allows us to evaluate your problem-solving approaches, communication clarity, architectural thinking, and familiarity with modern data practices.

---

## 📌 Instructions & Requirements

* **Format:** Provide **one single video link** using **Loom**, **YouTube (Unlisted)**, or **Google Drive (Access: Anyone with link can view)**.
* **Duration:** **1 to 3 minutes maximum per question** (Total video length: 5–15 minutes).
* **Language:** English (spoken).
* **Navigation:** You must include **timestamps / chapter sections** in the video description or response submission (e.g., `00:00 - Q1`, `02:15 - Q2`, etc.) corresponding to each question.
* **Setup:** Screen sharing or visual diagrams are optional but welcome if they help illustrate your answers.

---

## 🎥 Questions & Focus Areas

### 1. Problem Solving & Silent Pipeline Failures
**Scenario:** A data pipeline completes with an `Exit Code: 0` (success status) in your orchestrator, but zero rows were loaded into the destination warehouse, or incoming values were corrupted due to an upstream schema drift. No automated alert was triggered.

* **Question:** How do you approach detecting, debugging, and preventing "silent failures" in data pipelines?

---

### 2. Metadata & Catalog Optimization
**Scenario:** As a data team scales, searching for datasets, understanding data freshness, tracking column definitions, and managing query execution efficiency become major bottlenecks.

* **Question:** How do you design, optimize, and maintain a metadata management and cataloging strategy?

---

### 3. Data Standardization Strategies
**Scenario:** Your platform ingests data from disparate sources (APIs, third-party CSVs, CDC streams) with varying date formats, mixed case conventions, missing values, and inconsistent schema versions.

* **Question:** What is your end-to-end strategy for enforcing data standardization across ingestion and transformation layers?

---

### 4. Data Governance & Security Strategy
**Scenario:** Pipelines ingest data containing Personally Identifiable Information (PII) and financial records that require strict regulatory compliance (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA), role-based access controls, and auditability.

* **Question:** How do you implement robust data governance, access controls, and column/row-level security without hindering developer productivity?

---

### 5. AI & LLMs in Data Engineering
**Scenario:** The landscape of data engineering is evolving with AI-assisted tools and generative AI capabilities.

* **Question:** What is your view on the role of AI/LLMs in modern data engineering, and how do you practically leverage AI tools in your workflow?

---
